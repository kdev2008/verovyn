In plain words

Approvals before treatment protect payment after it.

For many procedures, the insurer wants to say yes before treatment, not after. That approval is called a prior authorization.

We check whether one is needed, collect the clinical documents, submit the request and follow up until there is an answer. The approval number and dates are recorded and linked to the appointment.

Your team knows what is approved, what is pending and what needs attention, so patients are scheduled with confidence.

A good fit if…

  • Procedures are delayed while approvals are chased
  • Staff are unsure which services need approval
  • Claims are denied for missing authorization
  • You want one place to see every request

FAQ

Prior Authorization: common questions.

Straight answers before you book a call.

Ask us anything
Which services need prior authorization?

It depends on the payer, plan and procedure. We verify requirements for each request.

Do you follow up with payers?

Yes. Pending authorizations are tracked and followed up until a decision.

Can you help with dental pre-treatment estimates?

Yes. Pre-treatment estimates and pre-determinations are supported for dental clients.

What if an authorization is denied?

A specialist reviews the reason and supports the next step, such as additional documentation or an appeal.

Can I check authorization status quickly?

Yes. Ask your client assistant or open your portal tracker.

Do you sign a Business Associate Agreement?

Yes. A BAA is signed before any protected health information is shared.
