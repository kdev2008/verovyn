PUBLIC WEBSITE INFORMATION

Website privacy overview

This static website does not have a connected enquiry backend by default. Information typed into the contact form is used in your browser to prepare an enquiry draft and is not transmitted to Verovyn by this website unless the site owner later connects a form submission service.

Do not submit patient data

Please do not enter protected health information (PHI), patient identifiers, clinical records, or sensitive billing records into a public website form.

Third-party services

This website can load web fonts from Google Fonts. If the site owner enables analytics or a hosted form service, the privacy documentation and configuration should be reviewed and updated before launch.

Important publication note

This page is a starter disclosure for the included static site, not legal advice or a finalized organization-wide privacy policy. The site owner should review and publish the appropriate policy before enabling data collection.
