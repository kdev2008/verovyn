In plain words

Clean claims get paid first time.

A claim is the formal request for payment. Small mistakes, such as a wrong ID, an old code or a missing attachment, make it bounce back, and every bounce costs days.

We check each claim against payer rules before it leaves, send it electronically, and watch for acknowledgement. If it is rejected, we correct it and send it again, usually within the same working day.

Every claim has a status and an owner. Nothing waits in a drawer.

A good fit if…

  • Claims are rejected for avoidable reasons
  • You do not know where submitted claims are
  • Corrections take too long
  • Payers have different rules and you cannot keep up

FAQ

Claims Submission: common questions.

Straight answers before you book a call.

Ask us anything
What is claim scrubbing?

It is a pre-submission check that catches coding, demographic, coverage and payer-rule errors before the claim is released.

How do you handle rejected claims?

Rejections are reviewed, corrected and resubmitted quickly, and recurring causes are shared with you.

Do you track timely filing deadlines?

Yes. Claims are monitored against each payer’s filing window.

Do you support both dental and medical claims?

Yes. Medical claims on professional formats and dental claims with CDT codes and attachments.

How will I see claim status?

Your portal shows claims by status, and your client assistant can answer on request.

Do you sign a Business Associate Agreement?

Yes. A BAA is signed before any protected health information is shared.
