In plain words

Providers cannot bill what they are not enrolled to bill.

Before a provider can bill a payer, the payer has to approve them. That means applications, license checks, a complete CAQH profile and a lot of waiting.

We gather the documents, complete the applications, and follow each payer until there is an effective date. We then keep a calendar of renewals so that nothing lapses quietly.

You get one tracker that shows every provider, every payer and the next step, instead of scattered emails and sticky notes.

A good fit if…

  • A new provider is joining and cannot bill yet
  • Your enrollment records are scattered
  • Revalidation dates slip past unnoticed
  • You are adding payers or locations

Ask us anything
How long does credentialing take?

It varies by payer and state. We keep applications moving and report status so you always know the next step.

Do you maintain CAQH profiles?

Yes. Profiles are kept complete and re-attested on schedule.

Can you enroll new providers joining a group?

Yes. We prepare and track applications for new providers across your payers.

Do you track license and certificate expiry?

Yes. Expiry dates are tracked and flagged ahead of time.

Do you support dental and medical providers?

Yes. Workflows differ by payer and specialty and are configured during onboarding.

Do you sign a Business Associate Agreement?

Yes. A BAA is signed before any protected health information is shared.
