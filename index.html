FAQ

Questions practices ask before outsourcing.

What is revenue cycle management (RCM)?

RCM is everything between a patient booking a visit and your practice being paid in full: verifying insurance, coding, sending claims, working denials, following up on A/R and posting payments. Verovyn handles these steps for medical and dental providers.

Do you provide both medical billing and dental billing services?

Yes. Medical and dental revenue cycles share a goal but have different codes, rules and documents, so each is configured separately by specialists who understand it.

Where is your team based?

Verovyn Health Services is an India-based company that serves US healthcare providers. Our processes are built around US payers, US coding standards and US HIPAA requirements.

Will we still see what is happening with our billing?

Yes. You get a clean portal for claims, A/R, denials and reports, and your own client AI assistant can answer questions and send PDFs at any time.

How does the AI assistant work?

Each client has its own assistant. Providers, front desk staff and managers can ask about patient status, insurance verification, urgent requests, A/R, payments or reports. Specialists review anything unusual and own every exception.

Is patient information protected?

We work under a signed Business Associate Agreement and follow HIPAA safeguards: encryption, role-based access and audit logs. See our HIPAA & Data Security page for details.

Can we start with only one service?

Yes. Many clients start with eligibility verification or A/R follow-up and add services later. You can also use the full revenue cycle.

How do we get started?

Book a short discovery call. We learn how your billing runs today, agree on scope and controls, and onboard in phases.

Book a short discovery call. We will learn how your billing runs today and show you what a more connected approach could look like.
