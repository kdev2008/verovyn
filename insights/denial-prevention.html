Denial prevention starts long before submission

Five upstream handoffs where strong process can help protect the revenue cycle.

Editorial guide · 4 min read · Verovyn Editorial

Start with accurate coverage details

Eligibility, benefit limits, payer-specific requirements, and demographic information affect the entire downstream process.

Connect documentation and coding

A claim may be complete on paper yet miss an attachment or payer detail. Defined review steps reduce avoidable rework.

Treat rejections as operational feedback

Segment issues by payer, provider, code, and cause rather than viewing them only as isolated events.

Give follow-up clear ownership

Age-based and exception-based worklists support consistent action and reduce ambiguity.

Review themes, not just totals

Trend analysis can help prioritize the underlying causes that create repeated problems.
