FAQ

Questions about our services.

Which RCM services does Verovyn offer?

Medical billing and coding, dental billing and collections, eligibility and benefits verification, provider credentialing, claims submission, denial management, A/R follow-up, payment posting, prior authorization and revenue analytics.

Can we choose only some services?

Yes. Many practices start with one or two services, such as eligibility verification or A/R follow-up, and add more later.

Do services work with our existing software?

Yes. We work inside your practice-management system or EHR wherever possible. Access is agreed during discovery.

Who will we work with day to day?

A named specialist owns your account, supported by our team and your client AI assistant.

Do you sign a Business Associate Agreement?

Yes. A BAA is signed before any protected health information is shared.
