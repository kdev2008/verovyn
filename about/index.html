About Verovyn

The intelligence of technology. The accountability of people.

Verovyn Health Services is an India-based revenue cycle management company serving US medical and dental providers, built on automation expertise and a clinician-informed perspective.

Our purpose

Healthcare teams should spend less energy chasing the revenue cycle.

Verovyn was built around a clear idea: billing work can be more connected, more transparent and better prioritized, without losing the people who understand healthcare best.

We focus on medical and dental revenue cycle management for US providers, combining disciplined processes, purposeful automation and professional review.

RCM company in India

Medical billing and dental billing company in India for US providers.

Verovyn Health Services provides medical billing, dental billing and complete revenue cycle management for US physician practices, dental clinics and DSOs, hospitals, specialty clinics and behavioral health providers. Our India-based specialists handle insurance eligibility verification, prior authorization, medical and dental coding review, claims submission, denial management and appeals, accounts receivable follow-up, payment posting, provider credentialing and revenue reporting, supported by a dedicated AI assistant for every client.
