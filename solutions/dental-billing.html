In plain words

Dental billing runs on benefits, limits and attachments.

Dental insurance is not like medical insurance. Most plans cap what they pay each year, limit how often a service is covered and want proof such as an X-ray before they approve certain work.

So we start before the patient sits down. We check the plan, note the limits and tell your front desk what is covered. After treatment we send the claim with the right attachments and follow it until the insurance portion is paid.

Whatever insurance does not cover becomes a clear patient balance, so your team has a clean number to share and nothing quietly ages in the background.

A good fit if…

  • Patients are surprised by what insurance does not cover
  • Claims are held up for missing radiographs or narratives
  • You run several locations and want one consistent process
  • Insurance and patient balances are hard to reconcile

What does dental billing and collections include?

Benefit verification, CDT coding review, attachments, claim submission and tracking, payment posting, insurance follow-up and patient balance reconciliation.

Do you handle attachments such as radiographs?

Yes. Radiographs, charting and narratives are matched to claims before submission, so payers receive what they need.

Can you support DSOs and multi-location dental groups?

Yes. One standard process across locations, with location-level and group-level reporting.

Which dental systems do you work with?

We work inside your practice-management system wherever possible. Access and workflow are agreed during discovery.

Do you verify benefits before treatment?

Yes. Verification includes plan details, annual maximum, deductible, frequency limits and waiting periods, shared before the visit.

Do you sign a Business Associate Agreement?

Yes. A BAA is signed before any protected health information is shared.
