In plain words

Accurate billing starts before the claim is built.

Think of a patient visit as a story that has to be told correctly to an insurance company. The doctor writes the clinical story. Billing turns it into codes. If the two do not match, the payer questions it, delays it or says no.

Our team reads the story and the codes side by side before anything goes out. If a note is thin, a modifier is missing or a diagnosis is not linked, we ask the provider a short, specific question and track it until it is answered.

You do not get a black box. You see which charges are waiting, which are coded and which are with a provider, and you can ask your client assistant at any time.

A good fit if…

  • You want fewer billing errors without hiring more back-office staff
  • Charges often reach claims late or incomplete
  • Providers lose time to billing questions
  • You want clear numbers on what is billed and what is paid

FAQ

Ask us anything
What medical billing services do you provide?

Charge review, coding review, claim preparation, submission, denial follow-up, A/R follow-up, payment posting and reporting. You can use one service or the full cycle.

Do you code from provider notes?

Certified coding professionals review charges against documentation. Anything unclear becomes a provider query, tracked until closed.

Which specialties do you support?

Primary care, internal medicine, multi-specialty groups and many specialties. Workflows are configured per specialty during onboarding.

Can you work inside our EHR?

Wherever possible we work inside your existing EHR or practice-management environment. Access is agreed during discovery.

How do we see what is happening?

Your portal shows charges, claims, denials and A/R, and your client assistant answers questions on request.

Do you sign a Business Associate Agreement?

Yes. A BAA is signed before any protected health information is shared.
