In plain words

A clean claim begins at the front desk.

Eligibility is simply the question: is this person covered today, and for what? It sounds small, but a wrong answer can undo a perfectly coded claim.

We verify before the appointment, record copay, deductible, coinsurance and limits, and flag anything unusual to a specialist. If a patient walks in unannounced, tell your assistant and we put that request first.

The result is a short, readable summary your front desk can use, and a PDF on request, so the conversation at check-in is calm and accurate.

A good fit if…

  • Front desk staff spend hours on hold with payers
  • Patients are surprised by their share of the bill
  • Same-day and walk-in patients need fast answers
  • You want benefits recorded the same way every time

FAQ

Eligibility Verification: common questions.

Straight answers before you book a call.

Ask us anything
How early do you verify eligibility?

We verify ahead of the appointment according to the schedule you share, and handle urgent requests the same day.

Can we ask for a result in the middle of the day?

Yes. Ask your client assistant, for example "Is Patient A insured?", and receive the answer and a PDF.

Do you verify dental and medical benefits?

Yes. Medical verification covers copay, deductible and coinsurance; dental verification adds annual maximum, frequency limits and waiting periods.

What if coverage is inactive?

A specialist reviews it, contacts the payer if needed and informs your front desk right away.

Do you share protected health information in chat?

Chats are limited to authorized users under a signed BAA. See our HIPAA & Data Security page.

Do you sign a Business Associate Agreement?

Yes. A BAA is signed before any protected health information is shared.
