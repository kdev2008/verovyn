Denial Management & Appeals

In plain words

Every denial is a signal and an opportunity.

A denial is not always the end. Often it simply means the payer needs something: a correct code, a document or a better explanation.

We sort denials by cause and by what is at stake, fix and resubmit what can be fixed, and prepare appeals with the right documents for the rest. Then we tell you why it happened so the front end can change.

Over time the same denial should appear less often. That is the point.

A good fit if…

  • Denials pile up and nobody owns them
  • The same denial reasons keep recurring
  • Appeal deadlines are missed
  • You want to know which payers and causes cost you most

FAQ

Denial Management: common questions.

Straight answers before you book a call.

Ask us anything
Do you appeal every denial?

We prioritize by value, cause and appeal deadline. Fixable denials are corrected; others are appealed with supporting documentation.

How do you stop denials recurring?

We report denials by cause and payer and agree front-end changes with your team.

Can you work old denials?

Yes. Older denials can be reviewed and worked where payer deadlines allow.

Who writes the appeals?

Specialists prepare appeals; AI helpers can organize documents and drafts for review.

Will I see denial trends?

Yes. Denials by cause, payer and procedure are in your portal.

Do you sign a Business Associate Agreement?

Yes. A BAA is signed before any protected health information is shared.
