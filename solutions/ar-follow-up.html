In plain words

Unworked A/R quietly becomes lost revenue.

Accounts receivable is the money that insurers and patients still owe you. The older it gets, the harder it is to collect.

Our team reviews it by age, payer and value, then works the most important claims first. We contact payers, correct what needs correcting and push stuck claims toward a decision.

You see aging in your portal, and you can ask your assistant how much has been collected whenever you need to know.

A good fit if…

  • A large share of your A/R is over 60 days
  • Nobody has time to chase payers
  • Underpayments go unquestioned
  • Patient balances are left after insurance pays

FAQ

A/R Follow-Up: common questions.

Straight answers before you book a call.

Ask us anything
How do you prioritize A/R follow-up?

By value, age and payer deadlines, so claims with the most at stake are worked first.

Can you work aged A/R?

Yes. Older balances can be reviewed and worked where payer deadlines allow.

Do you follow up on patient balances?

Yes, in line with the process and tone you set for your patients.

How is progress reported?

Aging, days in A/R and payer performance are in your portal, with PDF reports on request.

Can I ask about A/R at any time?

Yes. Ask your client assistant, for example "How much A/R have we collected?"

Do you sign a Business Associate Agreement?

Yes. A BAA is signed before any protected health information is shared.
